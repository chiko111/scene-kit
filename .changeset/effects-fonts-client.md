New effects with color cycles (1–4 colors): `glow` (letters glow on text nodes), `edgeBlink` (blink or fade) and `borderRun` (lights running around the border). Font selection for text and buttons via a `fonts` map. `createSceneClient()` — load/save/upload against two (or three) plain endpoints, with caching that is safe for React `use()`.
